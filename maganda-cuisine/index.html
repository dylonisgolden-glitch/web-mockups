SubicFlavors of Zambales

From a family kitchen in Subic

Maganda Cuisine began in February 2024 from our family's love for Filipino food and the flavors of home in Subic, Zambales. What started as cooking for friends became a food truck.

For us, cooking is a skill learned by heart, through family and tradition. We are proud to share a taste of our culture and bring comfort in every meal.
