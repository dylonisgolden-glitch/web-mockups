Un pedacito de Puerto Rico

El Guavate de Oto is a small family business in Riverview, Florida, cooking some of the most delicious Puerto Rican food in the Tampa Bay area. Everything is made with fresh local ingredients to bring you the taste of home.

Guests say stepping up to the truck feels like el campo in Puerto Rico, with the music, the fans and the food to match.

From the truck

Fan favorite

Mofongo

Mashed green plantain with crispy fried pork or chicken and broth, or stuffed with chicken or skirt steak.

Aperitivo

Alcapurrias

Crispy plantain fritters stuffed with a seasoned filling.

Plato del día

Arroz con gandules y lechón

Yellow rice with pigeon peas, roast pork and yuca.

Acompañante

Tostones

Twice-fried green plantains, crisp outside and tender inside.
